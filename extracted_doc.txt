1. Executive Summary
EduBridge is a hybrid ed-tech platform that connects school students (Classes 3 to 12)
with teachers and institutional administrators. It replaces scattered WhatsApp groups,
emails and paper homework with one secure system for study material, assignments,
grading, live classes and attendance.
The platform has three deliverables:
Supporting services: Redis (cache and queue), Celery workers, S3 or Cloudflare R2 (file
storage), Firebase Cloud Messaging (push), Jitsi Meet or Zoom Video SDK (live classes),
Nginx (reverse proxy and TLS), Docker Compose (deployment).
Delivery plan: Phase 1 (MVP) delivers student and admin flows with the full assignment
loop. Phase 2 adds push notifications and live classes with attendance. Phase 3 adds the
teacher portal, parent reports, offline caching and an AI doubt solver.
2. Problem Statement and Objectives
2.1 Problems being solved
1. Fragmented communication. Notes, homework and doubts are spread across
WhatsApp, email and paper, so nothing is searchable or auditable.
2. No central evaluation. Staff cannot see who submitted what and when, and feedback
is not structured or stored.

11.3 Classroom rules
Students join with microphone and camera off; only moderators can unmute them.
Lobby is disabled because the JWT already proves identity.
Chat is on, with a profanity filter applied by the moderator tools; the moderator can
disable chat or mute all.
Recording is optional. When enabled, recording_url is stored on the live class and
shown to the class after processing.
Low bandwidth: the app offers an audio-only toggle and lowers video to 180p
automatically after two connection drops.
11.4 Attendance logic
1. First join creates the row with joined_at and zero reconnects.
2. Each later join adds one to reconnects and clears left_at.
3. Leave adds (now minus last join time) to duration_seconds and sets left_at.
4. When the class ends, any open row is closed at the end time.
5. A student counts as present if total duration is at least 10 minutes. The threshold is a
setting.

12.2 Child data protection
EduBridge stores personal data of minors, so privacy rules apply from day one.
India (DPDP Act 2023): processing a child's data (under 18) needs verifiable consent
from a parent or guardian, and the Act prohibits tracking, behavioural monitoring and
targeted advertising aimed at children. Plan: the school collects consent at admission
and the admin records it per student; the app shows no ads and runs no analytics SDK
that profiles students.
COPPA (United States): applies only if the product is offered to children under 13 in
the US. It is listed in the original proposal for completeness; confirm applicability
before launch.
Data minimisation: store only name, phone, class, roll number and guardian contact.
No location, contacts or microphone data are collected outside live classes.
Retention: submissions and attendance are kept for the academic year plus one year,
then deleted or anonymised on school request.
Parent rights: a guardian can request a copy or deletion of the child's data through the
school admin; the admin tool must support export and delete for one student.
Legal review: these notes are engineering guidance. Have counsel confirm the consent
flow and retention policy before the pilot.

3. Run docker compose up -d to start db, redis, api, worker and nginx.

1. Admin creates class, subject, chapter and uploads a PDF; student of that class sees it,
student of another class does not.

photos; admin grades 22 of 25; student sees marks and remarks.
